How it works

Pick an outcome and a stake. The book quotes what your bet returns if that outcome wins, the stake times its odds, and your wallet signs exactly that: the stake leaves your balance into the market's pot at the casino, and the bet is final.

When the event is over, the book names the winner. The casino pays every winning bet from the pot, and the book's own bank there pays what the pot cannot; your wallet checks the book's signed result before it collects your winnings. A result is the book's word: nothing but its reputation stops it naming the wrong one. If it names none by the market's deadline, or its bank cannot pay, every bet is refunded.

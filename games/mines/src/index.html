How to play

Start with five tiles and one mine. Each reveal samples a new safe/mine outcome using the exact remaining probabilities: 4/5, 3/4, then 2/3 chance of a gem. Tile positions are your visual choices; no hidden board is pre-generated.

Cash out after one, two, or three gems for nominal returns of 1.20×, 1.56×, or 2.28× stake. A mine ends the round at zero nominal payout. The amount paid out reflects exact continuation pricing and fees; it can differ from the nominal return. The displayed continuation value is not a cash-out quote.

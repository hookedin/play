How to play

Choose your stake and how many of the 25 tiles hide mines, then bet. Pick tiles one at a time: a gem raises what you can cash out, a mine ends the round and takes the stake. Cash out after any gem. The more mines, the more each gem pays.

A cash-out pays 99% of fair odds for the gems found. There is no hidden board: each pick is a bet of its own at the exact odds of the tiles left, decided when you make it. At a large stake the casino may cover fewer picks than the board holds; the game says when you reach that point, and you cash out there.

Table rules

Unlimited decks. Dealer stands on soft 17 and checks for blackjack before you play. A natural blackjack pays 3:2; two naturals push. Ordinary wins pay 1:1 and ties return the bet.

Double on any first two cards, including after a split, and receive exactly one more card. Split an initial equal-value pair once into two hands with an additional equal bet. No re-splitting. Split aces receive one card each; a split 21 is not a natural blackjack. Both hands play against the same dealer.

Insurance is offered when the dealer shows an Ace. It costs half the initial bet and pays 2:1 if the dealer has blackjack. There is no surrender. Doubles, splits and insurance require enough additional money in the game balance.

With optimal play through a completed hand, the calculated house edge is 0.5703880123% per initial bet (99.4296119877% return on that basis), rounding to 0.57%. Commissions do not reduce the displayed payouts. Stake must be an even number of wei. Stopping through the wallet mid-hand is outside these completed-hand figures.

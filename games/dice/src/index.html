    How to play

    Set your win chance, from 10% to 90%, with the slider or by typing it, choose a stake and roll. The roll is a number from 0 to 100: under your win chance wins. A win pays the stake times the multiplier, which is 99 divided by the win chance; a loss pays nothing. Space rolls.

    Auto rolls 10, 50 or 100 times at the same stake and odds; press Stop to end it early. Your wallet checks each roll before it signs it, and your bet history keeps what each one paid.

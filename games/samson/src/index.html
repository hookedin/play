How to play

Set your bet and press Spin, or Space. Wins pay for three or more matching symbols on adjacent reels from the left, in any row, as the paytable lists. Only the best win on the screen pays, and every win is at least your bet.

Three honeycombs pay × your bet and offer bonus spins for it, on reels where wilds multiply wins; each bonus spin is one bet, and you may keep the prize instead. Buy bonus plays those spins at once. Auto plays up to 100 spins and stops at a bonus.

Your wallet checks every bet. A spin is at most one bet, your whole bet against one pay, drawn so that spins pay exactly as the reels do, and the reels stop on a position that pays the verified result.

Paytable

Jawbone wild lands on reels 2, 3 and 4 and stands in for every symbol but the honeycomb. On the bonus reels it multiplies: ×2 on reels 2 and 4, ×3 on reel 3, and up to ×12 together.

Honeycomb lands on reels 1, 3 and 5. Three pay × your bet on top of any win, and offer bonus spins for it, one bet each. Three more during the bonus pay the same and add spins.

  • Symbols pay per way, in bets, for 3, 4 or 5 adjacent reels from the left, in any row.
  • Ways multiply: two matching symbols on one reel and three on another make six ways.
  • Only the best win on the screen pays. Every win is at least your bet.

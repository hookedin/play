How it works

Every spin's odds are counted exactly from the reel strips shown in the paytable: nothing is simulated or rounded. This page turns each spin into at most one casino bet of two outcomes, your whole bet against one pay, drawn so that spins reach every pay exactly as often as the reels do, and a spin that pays back exactly your bet places none; that draw is this game's word, and each bet's own odds are the casino's. After the wallet verifies the result, the reels stop on a position drawn from exactly those that pay it, so reels and money together behave like the physical strips.

Only the best win on the screen is paid, and no win is smaller than your bet. Bonus spins are prepaid: the triggering spin pays their cost in cash, which you may keep instead. The casino can decline a casino bet it cannot back; your balance is then unchanged and the same spin is offered again.

Paytable

Jawbone wild lands on reels 2, 3 and 4 and stands in for every symbol except the honeycomb. On the bonus reels it carries ×2 (reels 2 and 4) or ×3 (reel 3); multipliers in a win multiply each other, up to ×12.

Honeycomb lands on reels 1, 3 and 5. All three pay your bet in cash on top of any win, and offer to spend it on bonus spins on the multiplier reels, one bet each. More honeycombs during the bonus add more spins the same way.

  • Wins pay for 3, 4 or 5 matching symbols on adjacent reels from the left, in any rows.
  • Pay = symbol pay × ways × wild multipliers, in bets. Ways multiply the matching symbols on each reel.
  • Only the best win on the screen is paid. Every win is at least your bet.

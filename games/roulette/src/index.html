How it works

Click a number or an outside bet to put a chip on it; right-click takes one off. All your chips are one bet, which your wallet signs whole: a number returns 36 for 1, a dozen or a column 3 for 1, and red, black, odd, even, low and high 2 for 1. Zero belongs to the house, which is the game's whole 2.7% edge.

Everyone at the table shares one spin. The wheel is the game's referee: it opens a pot at the casino with the hash of a seed it has drawn, and the casino names the pot by the hash of a secret it has already fixed. Your wallet enters the pot with your bet, which is final once it is in, and the wheel spins twenty seconds after the first chip is down. The casino then reveals the secret, the number is read from the secret and the seed together, and your wallet checks both before it collects what your bet won. The wheel never knew the secret, and the casino fixed it before there was a seed; together they could choose the number. A bet the casino declines costs nothing, and if the wheel does not spin in time every bet is refunded. Each asset has its own wheel: you join the one for what your wallet plays with.

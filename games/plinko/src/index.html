How it works

Each peg sends the ball left or right with equal chance, so a bucket's probability is its number of paths divided by 2rows, and its multiplier is what landing there pays. The white marks under the buckets show where that many balls should have landed; the bars show where yours did. Your wallet works out exactly what each bet is worth before it signs it, and your bet history keeps that figure.

A drop is one bet of two outcomes: this page keeps what your ball cannot lose and bets the rest between two buckets, drawn so that drops reach every bucket exactly as often as the pegs do. That draw is this game's word; the bet's own odds are the casino's, and its verified outcome picks the bucket and the path the ball takes into it. A bet the casino declines can simply be dropped again.

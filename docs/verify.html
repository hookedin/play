Check a HookedIn bet

This page works a casino bet out again from what its receipt keeps, with nothing but the arithmetic below: no wallet, no server and no library. Save it and open it offline if you like. A bet's Check it yourself link fills it in after #, which a browser never sends to a server.

What it works out

How

  • The casino fixed its round as keccak256(secret) before your wallet picked its seed, and your bet signed keccak256(seed) before the secret was out.
  • The outcome is the lowest 64 bits of keccak256(abi.encode(keccak256("HOOKEDIN/OUTCOME"), seed, secret)), and the roll is the outcome as a share of 264, on a scale of 0 to 100. The bet pays its prize when the outcome is below its chance.
  • A game's step is a table of results, each with a weight and the cash it leaves you. Your wallet splits it into ways, each a casino bet or none, that reach every amount exactly as often as the table says, and draws one from the seed before it signs: a number below the ways' total weight, read big-endian from as many bytes as it needs of keccak256(abi.encode(keccak256("HOOKEDIN/BRANCH"), seed, i)) for i = 0, 1, 2, …, the first byte cut to the bits it needs, and read again from the next bytes until it is below the total. The way drawn is the first whose running total of weights is above it.
  • Once the way has left you an amount, your wallet draws the result among those that leave it, in the same way from keccak256(abi.encode(keccak256("HOOKEDIN/RESULT"), seed, i)), by their weights. The next 64-bit number from those bytes is what the game shows the result with.

The same in one line with Foundry's cast, for the outcome:

cast to-dec 0x$(cast keccak $(cast abi-encode "f(bytes32,bytes32,bytes32)" $(cast keccak "HOOKEDIN/OUTCOME") $SEED $SECRET) | cut -c 51-)

Python's hashlib.sha3_256 is a different hash, not keccak256: it gives another answer. HookedIn's contract settles a disputed bet with this same keccak256 computation. What a check proves.

Create your walletBackup and keys

Your wallet is a passkey, which your device or password manager keeps and syncs to your other devices. Each passkey is a wallet of its own: if you made one before, sign in with it.

Sign in with its passkey, or import its key, to open this account on another device.

A passkey works only on this site. A key file works in any copy of this wallet, even one you run yourself, and anyone who has it can take everything its account holds.

Anyone who sees this key can take everything this wallet holds. Never paste it into a site or a message.

Deletes every account and record this wallet keeps in this browser, in every tab. A passkey's account opens again when you sign in with it; an account you hold only as a key file needs that file.

How an allowance works
  • The money stays in your balance. The allowance is the most this game may put at risk.
  • It can lose all of it, winnings included, on its bets and payments, and not a wei more.
  • What a round wins joins the allowance once the game has shown how the round ended.
  • Leaving the game takes back what is left: it starts with nothing when you come back. Change it any time.
  • No game says what it pays back: your wallet works out each casino bet's odds before it signs, and your bets show what a game really paid.

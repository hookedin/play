How an allowance works
  • The money stays in your balance. The allowance is the most this game may put at risk.
  • It can lose all of it, winnings included, on its bets and payments, and not a wei more.
  • What a round wins joins the allowance once the game has shown how the round ended.
  • Leaving the game takes back what is left: it starts with nothing when you come back. Change it any time.
  • No game says what it pays back: your wallet works out each casino bet's odds before it signs, and your bets show what a game really paid.
